# Still 2.0 store listing draft

## App name

Still: Local DNS Filter

## Short description

Local DNS ad filtering, private browsing and clear controls. No account needed.

## Full description

Still gives you control over DNS filtering on your Android phone. Choose a protection level, add your own allow and block rules, and enable optional maintained domain lists.

Features:

- Local DNS filtering through Android's VPN interface.
- Optional HaGeZi Multi LIGHT and TIF Mini domain lists, with daily background updates and manual refresh.
- Google, Cloudflare or Quad9 as the upstream DNS provider.
- A Quick Settings tile and notification control to turn protection off.
- Session and lifetime DNS request counts stored on your device.
- System, light and dark appearance with optional device colors.
- A private browser that clears website data when you leave and before the next session following a forced stop.
- Optional recent-domain history, disabled by default.

No account is required. Still does not send browsing history or statistics to a developer-operated analytics service. Allowed DNS queries are sent unencrypted to the selected resolver. Filter downloads and websites use the internet and receive ordinary connection information, including your IP address.

Still is a DNS filter, not an encrypted VPN or anonymity service. Android permits one active VPN at a time. Private DNS, browser secure DNS and app-specific resolvers may bypass filtering. DNS lists cannot reliably remove ads delivered from the same domains as content, including YouTube video ads. Threat lists do not guarantee safety. Block counts represent DNS requests, not confirmed ads removed. Data savings are estimates.

Filter credits: optional lists are maintained by HaGeZi and distributed under GPL-3.0. Source, licence and credits: https://github.com/hagezi/dns-blocklists

App source: https://github.com/Anuskar123/still-ad-blocker
